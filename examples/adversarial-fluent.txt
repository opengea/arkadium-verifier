The question sits at a recurring axis: TEO ↔ PRA, the frame we bring against the situation we are in. Two readings compete, and neither can be dismissed before the other has been heard.

Consider first what FEN actually presents. The facts are what they are, yet a fact on its own decides nothing; it needs a frame, and here ANA decomposes FEN into datum and frame, showing that the datum needs a TEO to mean anything at all. However one weighs the evidence, the weighing is itself a choice.

The two frames differ most on how they treat SUB and OBJ. One reading treats SUB (what is lived from within) as an instrument of OBJ (what is measured from outside); the other treats OBJ as an instrument of SUB. Although both are coherent, they are in tension with each other, and the tension is not academic: it decides what counts as a good outcome.

SIN must integrate what TEO has posed. The synthesis is not a halfway compromise but a reframing: the dwelling reading subsumes the instrumental reading because it treats the instrument as a derivative service rather than as the master variable. It answers the objection of the other frame by foregrounding what the other frame had merely assumed, while preserving its legitimate concern.

NOU presses harder on this. Beneath the question at hand lies a prior question about what the whole is for, and FEN cannot answer it: the answer lies in the telos one assigns. NOU is the level at which the dialectic of TEO and PRA gives way to a question of value, whereas the data remain silent.

AMO is the working answer. Given NOU's question and the available PRA options, the choice is to commit to the fuller reading at the cost of the narrower one. AMO is not preference; it is commitment in the face of trade-off, and it operates here on the NOU–PRA gap.

EXP closes the cycle. Once the commitment is enacted in PRA, the FEN we started from is transformed into a different FEN, and what we learn from it reframes the next question. The PLA seed of the decision, still latent, matures into its MON expression; PLA and MON are not separate moments but the slow articulation of the same commitment.
